GIFT FAQ

A few helpful details.

Know what’s included and what happens after you send the request.

What does the $447 gift include?

Two private 90-minute one-on-one sessions with Kevin: three hours of guided, hands-on AI building. The recipient chooses a small first project with Kevin and learns how to continue on their own.

Does the recipient need coding experience or a project idea?

No. The Kickstart is designed for beginners. They can bring a goal, a question, or just curiosity; Kevin will help them find a useful starting point.

How does payment and delivery work?

After you submit the request, Kevin will confirm the recipient and delivery details and send you a payment link for the full $447. The gift certificate is sent after payment. It can be sent to you or, if you provide their email, directly to the recipient.

How does the recipient schedule the sessions?

They contact Kevin to arrange two 90-minute sessions. Times are coordinated after purchase and depend on availability.

Can I give this to someone under 18?

Yes. For a recipient under 18, a parent or guardian should contact Kevin to arrange the sessions and be involved in the process.
