FOR STUDENTS & FAMILIES

A few things students and parents ask.

A low-pressure introduction, shaped around the student’s interests and starting point.

Does the student need coding experience or a project idea?

No. The sessions start with curiosity. Kevin helps the student choose a small, hands-on project that fits their interests and experience.

What will the student do in a session?

They’ll build alongside Kevin, try AI as a creative tool, and learn how ideas become a simple prototype. It’s guided practice, not a lecture.

Who should request a session?

For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian should start the conversation. College students who are 18 or older can contact Kevin directly.

Where can I see the price and payment details?

The pricing page explains the AI Build Kickstart and payment split. Parents can use the student inquiry form to ask Kevin about fit before deciding.
