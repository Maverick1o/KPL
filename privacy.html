Your privacy.

This page explains what information this site collects and how it is used.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Website analytics

This site offers Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use its pages and coaching offers. Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, referring page, browser and device details, approximate location, and interactions such as scrolls and link clicks. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect and process this information. The Analytics script loads only if you choose “Allow analytics” in the privacy banner; if you decline, Analytics does not load.

Analytics is configured with Google Signals and ad personalization disabled. The site sends event details such as the page type, the type and placement of a button clicked, and the destination hostname. It does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, requested appointment times, or the contents of form messages to Google Analytics.

For details about Google's data practices, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Booking and contact forms

If you send a booking request or contact message, the information you enter—including your name, email address, phone number, requested time, and any optional note—is sent through FormSubmit to Kevin Kirkland so he can respond to your inquiry. These form details are not sent to Google Analytics.

Your choices

Your choice is saved in this browser when storage is available, so the banner does not appear again. You can change it here: . You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Google provides a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics. Cookie and privacy choices available to you may depend on your location.

Questions

For questions about information submitted through this site, use the contact page.
