THE SHORT VERSION

Real projects taught me what works—and what doesn’t.

For the past three years, I’ve been exploring AI tools as they evolve and putting them to work on real software projects. Through a lot of trial and error, I’ve developed practical ways to give AI useful context, guide its work, catch mistakes, and keep a project moving.

I’ve spent 20 years building software, and I’ve personally built or helped teams build more than 50 apps and SaaS products. I run a software consulting business today. Coaching is something I chose to add because I enjoy sharing the methods I’ve learned and helping people get capable with tools that can open up new possibilities.

My goal isn’t to take your idea away and disappear with it. We’ll make a useful start together, and I’ll teach you how to keep developing it—with support available when you want a hand.
