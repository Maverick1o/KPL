THE SHORT VERSION

Real projects taught me what works—and what doesn’t.

I’ve spent thousands of hours putting AI tools to work on real software projects—not just trying prompts, but figuring out how to make the whole process deliver: shaping the work, providing the right context, catching mistakes, and keeping momentum when things get weird.

We’ll start with an idea you care about and make something real. I’ll share the playbook, explain the decisions, and help you leave with the skills and confidence to keep going on your own.
